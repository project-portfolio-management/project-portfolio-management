Welcome to Project Portfolio Management Discussions

Project Portfolio Management is open-source software for recording the plans an organization funds and managing the work within them: tasks, schedules, cost, resources, risks, and governance. It compares plans to find possible duplicates and shows the evidence for each match. Discussions are now open, so there is a place to ask questions, suggest ideas, and share what you build.

## What this means for you

- You can ask questions and get answers that stay findable for the next person.
- You can suggest ideas and discuss them before anything is built.
- Maintainers will post news here: releases, changes of direction, and anything that affects people running the software.

## Where to go for what

- **Questions** about installing, running, or using it: Q&A.
- **Suggestions** for features or changes: Ideas.
- **Something you built** with it or on it: Show and tell.
- **Introductions and anything else:** General.
- **Bugs:** [Issues](https://github.com/project-portfolio-management/project-portfolio-management/issues).
- **Security problems:** do not post them here; follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/project-portfolio-management/project-portfolio-management/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

Before you contribute code, please read [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/project-portfolio-management/project-portfolio-management/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). The project has one maintainer and no third-party audit; what production use would take is listed item by item on the [path to production](https://project-portfolio-management.github.io/en-us/path-to-production/).

# Personas

Who the main users are, written so a task, a screen or a sentence of copy can be checked against a real need. The
pattern comes from the DHCW Roadmap Editor's Product Lead persona (RE-12,
[lessons](../dhcw-roadmaps-editor-lessons/index.md), lesson 8).

**How to read them.** Each persona has three kinds of statement:

- **Recorded:** a fact that this repository already records, with the file that records it.
- **Owner answer `Pn-An`:** a numbered answer the owner has given, with its date. A task cites it by number
  ("P1-A2"). **As of 2026-11-14 no owner answer has been given**, so every persona's list of owner answers is empty
  and its questions are open.
- **To check with real users:** what no one has yet asked a person in the role.

Nothing here is a research finding. No user research has been done for this project, and none is claimed.

| Persona | File | Primary surface |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 Portfolio office lead | [portfolio-office-lead.md](portfolio-office-lead.md) | Portfolio views, duplicate review, the director pack |
| P2 Sponsor or director | [sponsor.md](sponsor.md) | The director pack, gates, the public product roadmap |
| P3 Project or product lead | [project-lead.md](project-lead.md) | A plan's tasks, risks, issues and roadmap |
| P4 Reader of a public product roadmap | [public-roadmap-reader.md](public-roadmap-reader.md) | The public reader (RE-4), not the operator UI |

The roles in [§3](../03-stakeholders-and-users.md) are wider. These four are the ones a screen is designed for.

**To add an owner answer:** add it under the persona's *Owner answers* with the next number and the date, change
the matching open question to point at it, and cite it from the task.

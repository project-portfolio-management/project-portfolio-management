# For the Chief Executive Officer

*Chief Executive Officer: what this software is, what it gives you, what it does not, and the decisions that are yours.*

## What it is, in one paragraph

A registry of every **plan** the organization funds (portfolios, programmes, projects, products and so on, one recursive record), with a project-management workspace underneath (tasks, schedule, cost, resources, risks, issues, change requests, governance). It finds duplicate initiatives by explainable matching, so two departments chartering the same piece of work show up before both are funded. See [purpose and vision](../../../../spec/01-purpose-and-vision.md).

## What you can rely on

- **A single place to ask "what are we doing?"** Plans nest to any depth; private plans stay out of every total and count, for everyone, by a rule that tests check.
- **A monthly director pack** that is drafted once, issued by a named person, then **frozen by the database**: an issued pack cannot be edited; a correction is a new, reissued version with a note. Today it carries the portfolio map by strategic aim, delivery confidence, exceptions, issues and change requests.
- **Delivery confidence stated by people, shown beside the computed status, never blended with it.** A plan nobody has assessed shows "not assessed", never green. An amber-red or red rating opens an exception with a 48-hour clock (configurable); a missed clock is shown in the pack.
- **Decisions leave a trail.** Every change is audited, the audit trail cannot be altered through the service, and a change request cannot be approved by the person who raised it.

## What it does not give you yet

- The pack does **not** yet include gate decisions, funding and financial-year money, the buffer fever chart, earned value, validated benefits or the capacity heatmap. They are listed as `DP-5` to `DP-10` in [`tasks.md`](../../../../tasks.md), and the pack says which sections are not yet built rather than leaving a blank.
- No PDF or screen for the pack: it is delivered as data (`DP-11`).
- It is **not certified or audited** by anyone. The project's own verdict: ready for a controlled pilot on non-clinical, non-secret data, not for unsupervised production ([production readiness](../../../../governance/production-readiness.md)).
- One maintainer, no service-level agreement ([governance](../../../../GOVERNANCE.md)).

## Decisions that are yours

1. Whether to pilot, and on which part of the portfolio.
2. Who is the **sponsor** for the 48-hour exception rule and who may issue the monthly pack.
3. Whether to fund the gaps above (assessment, penetration test, support arrangement) or accept them.

# For the Chief People Officer

*Chief People Officer: what the software records about your people, how it protects them, and what it does for joiners, movers and leavers.*

## What it records about people

People appear as **opaque references**, not names or contact details, in assignments, owners, allocations, availability and leave notes, timesheets and recorded time, cost rates (finance-restricted), comments and approvals, and in the audit trail. Each user may set a personal notification destination (an e-mail address or chat webhook). Every such column is in the [personal-data inventory](../../../../governance/personal-data-inventory.csv).

Because it combines assignments, time, availability and cost, it is **monitoring-adjacent**, so a data protection impact assessment is expected ([DPIA](../../../../governance/dpia.md)); consult your staff representatives as the DPIA prompts.

## Protections for people

- **Cost rates** are visible only to finance-restricted roles.
- **The director pack shows owners by role, never by person,** and carries no person references.
- **Capacity and workload** are derived from records people or managers enter; they are not a performance score.
- **Erasure and access:** an administrator can export or pseudonymise one person's data after a preview. Pseudonymisation blanks who drafted or assessed a frozen record; it does not delete the record.
- **Separation of duties:** nobody approves their own change request.
- **Retention:** proposed periods for people-linked records are in the [retention schedule](../../../../governance/retention-schedule.md); only part is enforced automatically.

## Joiners, movers and leavers

Onboarding and offboarding cases carry checklists with an owner, and the access list can be reviewed to check that leavers' grants are removed ([assurance checklist](../../../../governance/assurance-checklist.md), item 11). It reports what was recorded in the service, not what your identity provider enforces; compare the two.

## Not covered

No HR record system, payroll, performance management or special-category data is in scope. Names typed into free text are not found by the data-subject tool. A capacity heatmap by skill pool for directors is not built (`DP-10`). Translations (Welsh included) are drafts.

## Decisions that are yours

What staff are told, the lawful basis for recording time and availability, how long people-linked records are kept, and who may see cost rates.

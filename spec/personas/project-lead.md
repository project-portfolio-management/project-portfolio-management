# P3 Project or product lead

- **Role:** owns one or more plans: their goals, tasks, issues, risks and, for a product, its public product roadmap.
  Not a developer.
- **Status:** draft, 2026-11-14 (RE-12). No owner answers yet. Not checked with anyone in the role.

## Recorded

- Owns a plan's goals, tasks and issues, assigns work, tracks the timeline ([§3](../03-stakeholders-and-users.md)).
- A product can have more than one lead, so two people can edit one roadmap; neither may overwrite the other (RE-2,
  from the DHCW persona's answer A12, which is DHCW's owner's answer, not this project's).
- The DHCW Product Lead spends about 30 minutes a week on roadmaps and treats six weeks untouched as stale (DHCW
  answers A1 and A7). Whether that holds here is open.

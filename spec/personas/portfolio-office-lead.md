# P1 Portfolio office lead

- **Role:** runs the portfolio office (PMO) of a large public body: registers plans, keeps the portfolio's
  hierarchy true, finds duplicated or overlapping initiatives, and prepares the board's papers.
- **Status:** draft, 2026-11-14 (RE-12). No owner answers yet. Not checked with anyone in the role.

## Recorded

- The audience is large health and government bodies, with non-clinical, non-secret data
  ([governance/production-readiness.md](../../governance/production-readiness.md)).
- Their interest is one registry of plans and their containment trees, and finding duplicates
  ([§3](../03-stakeholders-and-users.md)).
- A computed health score is refused: status is a person's judgement over published numbers (NC-4, BR-2 in
  [tasks.md](../../tasks.md)).
- Work arrives in spreadsheets, and the bulk import has a dry run ([bulk-import-export.md](../bulk-import-export.md)).
